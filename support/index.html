JourneyReady Support

JourneyReady is an independent study aid for Alberta welder apprentices (Periods 1–3) and anyone preparing for the interprovincial Red Seal Welder (456A) exam. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Alberta Apprenticeship and Industry Training (AIT), the Government of Alberta, the Red Seal Program / CCDA, Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC), NAIT, SAIT or any school or government body. Our practice questions are original, not official exam questions, and we can't guarantee exam results.

Contact: support@journeyready.ca. We aim to reply within 3 business days.

Report a mistake in a question or lesson

On a lesson, tap the report icon in the top bar. On a quiz question, after the answer and explanation are shown, tap Report a problem. During a timed exam the button appears when you review the questions after you submit.

The app opens a pre-filled email to support@journeyready.ca, or a Share / Copy sheet if Mail isn't set up. You send it yourself. The app does not send the message in the background. If the button isn't on the item you're looking at, email us and include the first line of the question or the lesson name, plus the version in Settings → About.

Please include:

  1. What you think is wrong, and your source if you have one (code, standard, textbook)
  2. App version (Settings → About)

Please don't send us real exam questions or any confidential exam material. If you do, we'll delete it.

Beta testers

  • Thank-you code: if you tested the TestFlight beta, email support@journeyready.ca and quote the code word from the TestFlight "What to Test" notes to claim a free thank-you code. Codes are limited (50 in total). We use your email only to send the code; we won't add you to a mailing list.

Purchases

  • Restore Purchases: open JourneyReady → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. This needs a network connection. Once restored, unlocks stay available offline.
  • Refunds: Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple's refund help). We can't issue refunds or see your payment details.
  • Family Sharing: in-app purchases are not shared with Family Sharing members.
  • What each purchase unlocks: a Period Pass unlocks one period (the Period 3 Pass also includes one full-length, fixed 125-question 456A practice exam with an optional 4-hour timer); All-Access unlocks all three periods plus the full 456A practice bank. There is no subscription and no account. Prices are shown in the app by Apple.

Common questions

  • Does it need internet? Lessons, quizzes, practice exams, review, games, calculators and any recorded lesson audio already on your phone work offline after install. You need a connection to buy or restore a pass, to download the optional AI Professor model (about 1.3 GB; Wi-Fi recommended), or to open Privacy Policy, Terms or Support in your browser. Once you have bought a pass, it stays unlocked offline.
  • What is the AI Professor? An optional on-device tutor that answers from the lessons on your phone. The Professor can be wrong. Check the lesson, your instructor and your apprenticeship authority. It is not official advice. On a phone with Apple Intelligence it can write answers on the device. Otherwise you can download the Professor pack (about 1.3 GB, Wi-Fi recommended) from the Professor tab or Settings; after that it also runs on the device and works offline. If the pack isn't downloaded, the Professor still shows relevant lesson text. You can type or dictate a question; dictation stays on the device.
  • How does lesson audio work? Every lesson has a Listen button. A small set of lessons include recorded narration bundled with the app; every other lesson is read by Apple's on-device voice. There is no separate "download audio" button. Lesson narration in JourneyReady uses an AI-generated voice. If audio won't play, check the silent switch and the volume, then try again. If it still fails, email us your app version and the lesson name.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is stored on your device only. It may be included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you use them. There's no account or cloud sync. Purchases follow your Apple Account; use Restore Purchases on a new phone.
  • How do I reset my progress? Settings → Erase all study progress. This can't be undone. It does not cancel purchases.
  • Android? No. JourneyReady is an iPhone app (iOS 18 or later).
  • Is this the official exam? No. JourneyReady is not an official exam, and our practice questions are original, not real exam questions. They're written to the published curriculum topics. Always check current exam information with your provincial apprenticeship authority; in Alberta, that's Tradesecrets (tradesecrets.alberta.ca), which also covers Alberta period exams and exam booking. For official sample questions, the self-assessment and the Exam Preparation Guide, see your trade's page on red-seal.ca.

Privacy. The app does not send us your study data, and your progress stays on your iPhone. If you email us, we use your message only to help you. See the Privacy Policy. Emails to support@journeyready.ca are received and stored by US-based providers; see section 9 of the Privacy Policy.
