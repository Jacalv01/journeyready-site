Prepare for your welder period exam. Study anywhere, even with no signal.

JourneyReady is an independent, offline iPhone study aid for Alberta welder apprentices (Periods 1–3) and the interprovincial 456A exam: lessons, quizzes, timed practice exams and flashcards.

Free TestFlight beta (iPhone): public link coming soon

18+ only. Beta testers must be 18 or older. By joining you confirm you're 18+.

Independent study aid. Not affiliated with AIT, the Government of Alberta, the Red Seal Program/CCDA or ESDC. Original practice questions; no pass guarantee.

Who it's for

Period 1–3 apprentices
Follows the section and topic structure of Alberta's published 2026 Welder curriculum guide.
Writing the 456A
A 125-question timed practice exam, block-weighted, with flagging and review.
On site or in camp
Everything is on your phone. No account and no signal needed.

What's inside

Lessons
73 plain-language lessons with diagrams and audio narration.
Topic quizzes
An explanation for every answer, plus "exam trap" callouts.
Period practice exams
Modelled on the published exam format: 100 questions · 3-hour timer · 70% target.
456A practice exam
125 questions, timed, flag and review.
Remember it
Spaced-repetition review and flashcards.
Shop calculators
Heat input, bend allowance, filler consumption, sling load and more. Free.

FAQ

Who makes JourneyReady? Is it connected to an exam authority? JourneyReady is an independent study aid made by Jerson Antonio Cruz-Alvarez in Alberta. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Alberta Apprenticeship and Industry Training (AIT), the Government of Alberta, the Red Seal Program/CCDA, Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC), NAIT, SAIT or any school or government body. For official sample questions, the self-assessment and the Exam Preparation Guide, see your trade's page on red-seal.ca. For Alberta period exams and exam booking, see tradesecrets.alberta.ca.

Are the questions taken from past exams? No. Every question is written to the published curriculum topics. Our practice questions are original, not official exam questions, and we can't guarantee exam results. Please don't send us real exam questions or any confidential exam material. If you do, we'll delete it.

Does it work offline? Yes. All content is on your device.

Android? Not yet. iPhone first.

I found a mistake. Email support@journeyready.ca. Confirmed errors are fixed in the next update.
